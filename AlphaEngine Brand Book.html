01 — Strategy & Language · Product terminology

Product terminology.

Use these terms in product and brand copy. CapitalScore is allocation-facing. ContestScore is incentive, prize, and anti-sybil facing.

01Strategy Arena

The competitive environment where strategies are submitted, evaluated, ranked, and compared.

02Encrypted strategy

A strategy specification protected during submission and evaluation.

03CapitalScore

The allocation-facing score used for selection and portfolio construction.

04ContestScore

The incentive-facing score used for prizes, rewards, and anti-sybil handling.

05Epoch

A competition period with fixed rules, datasets, costs, and scoring configuration.

06Robust utility

Risk-adjusted, cost-aware performance across scenarios, not raw APY.

07Benchmark Hold

The baseline used to test whether selected strategies add value.

08Reproducible artifacts

Hashes and metadata that verify results without exposing strategy logic.

09Capital progression

The path by which strong evidence can create eligibility for future allocation.

02 — Logo · Full lock-up

The horizontal lock-up

AlphaEngine
PRIMARY · horizontalPreferred

The lock-up pairs the fixed mark with the wordmark at a fixed ratio. The divider rule sits at the optical center; the wordmark cap-height equals the mark's inner counter.

  • →Mark height = wordmark cap-height × 1.8
  • →Gap between mark and rule = 0.5× mark height
  • →Use the stacked variant only below 120px wide
In product chrome the wordmark may be omitted — the mark alone is sufficient once the brand is established on screen.

02 — Logo · Mark
CONSTRUCTED ON A 48PX GRID

One stroke,
two ideas

The mark is an æ ligature drawn as a single unbroken line. The looping left form reads as the engine — a turning rotor — while the open right tail reads as forward motion and continuous iteration.

Engine
The closed loop — compute, simulate, repeat.
Alpha
The open tail — edge extracted, carried forward.

Stroke weight is uniform. Never outline, fill the counters, or alter the line caps.

Teal on near-black is primary. On busy or light surfaces, fall back to a solid knockout. Never place the teal mark on a mid-tone where contrast drops.

02 — Logo · Misuse

Don't do these

✕Don't recolor
The mark is teal — or a neutral knockout. Never tint it another hue.
✕Don't add the iridescent gradient
Iridescence is the confidential layer, not the brand mark. Keep them separate.
✕Don't distort
Scale proportionally only. Never stretch, squash, or skew.
✕Don't add effects
No glows, shadows, bevels, or outlines on the mark.
✕Don't low-contrast
Never place teal on a mid-tone. Use a knockout instead.
✕Don't crowd
AlphaEngine
Respect the clear-space zone — keep type and edges out.

05 — Patterns

Quiet fields, one loud field

Void is the default canvas. Grid is for structure, dot-field is for quiet depth, scanline is for evaluation, and iridescence is for confidential computation. Never use pattern to compensate for weak layout.
