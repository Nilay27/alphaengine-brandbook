Audience language

Same brand, different entry points.

Strategy builders

They care about: protecting edge, fair evaluation, reputation, rewards, and capital access.

Say: Submit private strategies. Compete on proof. Earn a path to capital.

Agents

They care about: constrained strategy surfaces, reproducible feedback, objective scoring, and iteration loops.

Say: Generate strategies, test them reproducibly, and improve through evidence.

Allocators

They care about: benchmark context, risk controls, persistence, capacity, and artifacts.

Say: Allocate through strategies selected under reproducible, risk-aware evaluation.

Protocols

They care about: better strategy coverage, liquidity routing, composable guardrails, and capital efficiency.

Say: Turn fragmented strategy supply into an open competition layer for on-chain finance.

Do not make every audience message sound like the same homepage headline. Lead with the audience’s decision context.

×Avoid

Hype without mechanism.

Claim boundaries

Performance language must carry context.

Never say

  • Yield promises without assumptions
  • No-downside return language
  • Alpha guarantees
  • Privacy without limits
  • APY-only ranking
  • Capital as an automatic outcome
  • Returns without benchmark context
  • Passive allocation language

Prefer

  • Strategies are evaluated under reproducible scoring.
  • Winners are selected for robust utility, not one-window APY.
  • Privacy protects strategy logic during evaluation.
  • Capital progression depends on persistence, risk, capacity, and guardrails.
  • Benchmark competitiveness must be shown net of conservative costs.

When discussing performance, include benchmark, period, assumptions, costs, and risk context.

Product terminology

User-facing terms, not formulas.

These terms keep PMF language accurate without exposing implementation detail. CapitalScore is allocation-facing. ContestScore is incentive, prize, and anti-sybil facing.

Strategy Arena

The competitive environment where strategies are submitted, evaluated, ranked, and compared.

Encrypted strategy

A strategy specification protected during submission and evaluation.

CapitalScore

The allocation-facing score used for selection and portfolio construction.

ContestScore

The incentive-facing score used for prizes, rewards, and anti-sybil handling.

Epoch

A competition period with fixed rules, datasets, costs, and scoring configuration.

Robust utility

Risk-adjusted, cost-aware performance across scenarios, not raw APY.

Benchmark Hold

The baseline used to test whether selected strategies add value.

Reproducible artifacts

Hashes and metadata that verify results without exposing strategy logic.

Capital progression

The path by which strong evidence can create eligibility for future allocation.

01 — Logo · Mark
CONSTRUCTED ON A 48PX GRID

One stroke,
two ideas

The mark is an æ ligature drawn as a single unbroken line. The looping left form reads as the engine — a turning rotor — while the open right tail reads as forward motion and continuous iteration.

Engine
The closed loop — compute, simulate, repeat.
Alpha
The open tail — edge extracted, carried forward.

Stroke weight is uniform. Never outline, fill the counters, or alter the line caps.

01 — Logo · Misuse

Don't do these

✕Don't recolor
The mark is teal — or a neutral knockout. Never tint it another hue.
✕Don't add the AI gradient
Iridescence is the confidential layer, not the brand mark. Keep them separate.
✕Don't distort
Scale proportionally only. Never stretch, squash, or skew.
✕Don't add effects
No glows, shadows, bevels, or outlines on the mark.
✕Don't low-contrast
Never place teal on a mid-tone. Use a knockout instead.
✕Don't crowd
