Brand statement

A trading terminal that feels like precision, and an intelligence layer that feels like magic.

AlphaEngine is a simulation workbench for algorithmic strategies on Pendle yield markets. The brand has two voices held in tension — the disciplined near-black terminal, and the iridescent promise of AI. This book is the contract that keeps them coherent.

The horizontal lock-up

AlphaEngine
PRIMARY · horizontalPreferred

The lock-up pairs the fixed mark with the wordmark at a fixed ratio. The divider rule sits at the optical center; the wordmark cap-height equals the mark's inner counter.

  • →Mark height = wordmark cap-height × 1.8
  • →Gap between mark and rule = 0.5× mark height
  • →Use the stacked variant only below 120px wide
In product chrome the wordmark may be omitted — the mark alone is sufficient once the brand is established on screen.

01 — Logo · Mark
CONSTRUCTED ON A 48PX GRID

One stroke,
two ideas

The mark is an æ ligature drawn as a single unbroken line. The looping left form reads as the engine — a turning rotor — while the open right terminal reads as forward motion and continuous iteration.

Engine
The closed loop — compute, simulate, repeat.
Alpha
The open tail — edge extracted, carried forward.

Stroke weight is uniform. Never outline, fill the counters, or alter the line caps.

01 — Logo · Color variations

Teal on near-black is primary. On busy or light surfaces, fall back to a solid knockout. Never place the teal mark on a mid-tone where contrast drops.

01 — Logo · Misuse

Don't do these

✕Don't recolor
The mark is teal — or a neutral knockout. Never tint it another hue.
✕Don't add the AI gradient
Iridescent is the AI layer, not the brand mark. Keep them separate.
✕Don't distort
Scale proportionally only. Never stretch, squash, or skew.
✕Don't add effects
No glows, shadows, bevels, or outlines on the mark.
✕Don't low-contrast
Never place teal on a mid-tone. Use a knockout instead.
✕Don't crowd
AlphaEngine
Respect the clear-space zone — keep type and edges out.

02 — Color · The AI iridescent

AI is iridescent,
never teal

Anywhere the product infers, generates, explains, or chats, the accent language switches to a 135° teal → violet → pink gradient with a slow shimmer. It is the visual promise of intelligence — and it is rationed as carefully as the brand teal.

05 — Iconography

Line icons,
one accent

Icons are 1.8px uniform stroke, rounded caps and joins, on a 24px grid. Default to --ae-text-secondary; switch to brand teal only when active. The AI sparkle is the single exception — it always carries the iridescent fill.
